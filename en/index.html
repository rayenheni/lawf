Frequently asked questions

Your questions, our answers.

Another question?
How does the first consultation work?

By appointment, at the office or remotely: we listen, qualify the facts and present your options and a strategy — a first orientation that commits you to nothing.

How are fees set?

Fees are agreed in writing upfront: flat fee, hourly rate or a success fee within the limits allowed by the profession's regulations. A quote is given before any intervention — no surprises.

Can I follow my case remotely from abroad?

Yes: secure exchange of documents, a report after every hearing and a properly framed power of attorney for procedural steps.

What confidentiality guarantees do I have?

Professional secrecy is absolute under the Tunisian law governing attorneys. This site stores none of your data: your message goes straight through WhatsApp or your own mail client.
